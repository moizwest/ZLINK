About Z-Link

Your premises. Our own crews.

Z-Link started more than 15 years ago supplying labour to other contractors. Today we run our own skilled team from a workshop in Al Sajaa, Sharjah, and deliver complete technical packages for facilities managers, factories, property owners and main contractors.

The engineer who surveys your site is part of the team that builds it. That keeps prices honest, programmes realistic and quality consistent.

    FAQ

    Questions clients ask us.

    Straight answers before you call.

    Which areas of the UAE do you cover?

    We work across all seven Emirates. Most of our projects are in Dubai and Sharjah, close to our office in Hor Al Anz and our workshop in Al Sajaa.

    Do you visit the site before quoting?

    Yes. We inspect, measure and test on site, then send an itemised quotation naming the system, method and programme. VAT is shown separately.

    Can you work while our building or factory stays open?

    Yes. We phase work around live operations, with barricades, signage and permits, and we can plan night or weekend shifts where needed.

    Do you subcontract the work?

    Our eight core trades are carried out by our own crews. Where a project needs a specialist outside these trades, we tell you at quotation stage.

    How quickly will you respond?

    We reply to every enquiry within 24 hours on working days with a survey date or a quotation.
