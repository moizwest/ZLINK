Our services in Sharjah

Waterproofing

Roofs, basements, wet areas, water tanks and podiums, plus leak investigation and repair on buildings that stay in use. Our own crews survey, apply and hand over.

Epoxy Flooring

Hard-wearing epoxy floors for car parks, warehouses, factories and food areas, with self-levelling and anti-slip finishes, coving and safety line marking, laid by our own crews.

Industrial Coatings

Protective and anti-corrosion coating systems for storage tanks, pipework, steel structures and plant, and GRP lining, applied at our Al Sajaa workshop in Sharjah or on site.

Civil Works

Concrete repair, crack injection, blockwork, drainage and demolition, done neatly around occupied spaces by our own civil team.

Painting

Interior and exterior painting for villas, apartments, towers, offices and restaurants: bold colours, accent and feature walls, texture and decorative finishes, and dual-tone facades.

Fire Systems

Fire pump suction line replacement, fire door overhaul and fire-rated (intumescent) coatings. Works that need Dubai Civil Defence approval are carried out with DCD-approved partners.

Landscaping

Soft and hard landscaping for villas, communities and campuses: palms and lawns, interlock paving, sports grounds and water features.

Turnkey Fit-out

Offices, washrooms, warehouses and post-fire reinstatement, coordinated by one contractor and handed over ready to use.

Questions clients ask

Do you visit the site before quoting?

Yes. We inspect, measure and test on site, then send an itemised quotation naming the system, method and programme. VAT is shown separately.

Can you work while our building or factory stays open?

Yes. We phase work around live operations, with barricades, signage and permits, and we can plan night or weekend shifts where needed.
