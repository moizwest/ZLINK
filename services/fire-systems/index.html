Fire Systems

Fire pump lines, fire doors and intumescent coatings

Fire pump suction line replacement, fire door overhaul and fire-rated (intumescent) coatings. Works that need Dubai Civil Defence approval are carried out with DCD-approved partners.

Get a quote+971 55 949 5833

Pipework

Replacement of fire pump suction lines and fire mains in HDPE or GI, including fittings and valves, planned so the system is out of service for the shortest possible time.

Fire doors

Maintenance, coating and general overhaul of fire exit doors and their hardware.

Intumescent coatings

Fire-rated intumescent coating of structural steel, applied to the specified thickness.

Approvals

Where a scope needs Dubai Civil Defence approval, we deliver it with DCD-approved partners and say so at quotation stage.

Planning around a live system

Fire protection has to stay available. We agree the isolation window with the facility team, prepare fittings in advance and restore the system the same day wherever the scope allows.

How we run a fire systems job

  1. Site survey. We inspect, measure and test on site before pricing anything.
  2. Quotation. Itemised scope, named system, method and programme. VAT shown separately.
  3. Execution. Our own crews, Monday to Saturday, phased around your operations.
  4. Handover. Joint inspection, snag closure and handover documents, with the warranty terms set out in the quotation.

Safe sites, every shift

Full PPE on every site, a daily toolbox talk before work starts, a risk assessment and method statement for every scope, and barricades, signage and permits around your operations.

Questions clients ask

Yes. We phase work around live operations, with barricades, signage and permits, and we can plan night or weekend shifts where needed.
