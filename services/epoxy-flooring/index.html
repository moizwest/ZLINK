Epoxy Flooring

Epoxy flooring contractor in Dubai and Sharjah

Hard-wearing epoxy floors for car parks, warehouses, factories and food areas, with self-levelling and anti-slip finishes, coving and safety line marking, laid by our own crews.

Get a quote+971 55 949 5833

Floors we lay

Warehouse and logistics floors, factory production areas, car parks and ramps, walkways and food-area floors. We match the system to the traffic: forklifts and pallet trucks, cars, foot traffic or wet processes.

Preparation decides the result

We diamond-grind or otherwise prepare the concrete, repair cracks and joints, then apply primer and the specified build. Anti-slip floors get an aggregate broadcast; coving, line marking and bay marking are finished in the same programme.

Working in live facilities

Most of our floors are laid in buildings that keep running. We phase areas so operations continue, with barricades and signage, and plan night or weekend shifts where needed.

Trained on the system

Our application team completed Sika U.A.E.'s Basic Application Training for Sikafloor® MultiFlex Traffic 1330 and Sikafloor®-1205 on 31 December 2025. This is basic application training; Z-Link is not a Sika approved applicator.

How we run a epoxy flooring job

  1. Site survey. We inspect, measure and test on site before pricing anything.
  2. Quotation. Itemised scope, named system, method and programme. VAT shown separately.
  3. Execution. Our own crews, Monday to Saturday, phased around your operations.
  4. Handover. Joint inspection, snag closure and handover documents, with the warranty terms set out in the quotation.

Safe sites, every shift

Full PPE on every site, a daily toolbox talk before work starts, a risk assessment and method statement for every scope, and barricades, signage and permits around your operations.
