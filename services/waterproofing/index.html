Waterproofing

Waterproofing contractor in Dubai and Sharjah

Roofs, basements, wet areas, water tanks and podiums, plus leak investigation and repair on buildings that stay in use. Our own crews survey, apply and hand over.

Where we waterproof

We work on warehouse and factory roofs, building podiums and terraces, basements, bathrooms and other wet areas, and water tanks. Many jobs start with a leak: we trace the source on site before proposing a repair, so you pay for the fix the building needs, not a blanket replacement.

Systems we apply

We select polyurethane, bituminous or cementitious systems to suit the substrate, exposure and use, and apply them to the manufacturer's specification. Joints, upstands, drains and penetrations get the detailing attention, because that is where most roofs fail.

How a waterproofing job runs

Survey and leak investigation, an itemised quotation naming the system and programme, surface preparation, application, then a flood or water test where the detail allows it before handover.

How we run a waterproofing job

  1. Site survey. We inspect, measure and test on site before pricing anything.
  2. Quotation. Itemised scope, named system, method and programme. VAT shown separately.
  3. Execution. Our own crews, Monday to Saturday, phased around your operations.
  4. Handover. Joint inspection, snag closure and handover documents, with the warranty terms set out in the quotation.

Safe sites, every shift

Full PPE on every site, a daily toolbox talk before work starts, a risk assessment and method statement for every scope, and barricades, signage and permits around your operations.
