Industrial Coatings

Industrial coatings and GRP lining in the UAE

Protective and anti-corrosion coating systems for storage tanks, pipework, steel structures and plant, and GRP lining, applied at our Al Sajaa workshop in Sharjah or on site.

Get a quote+971 55 949 5833

What we coat

Storage tanks, pipework, steel structures, brackets and fabricated steel for façade and metal fabricators, and plant items. Water tanks can be lined with GRP.

How we coat

Surface preparation including blast cleaning, then primers and topcoats applied to the specified dry-film thickness. DFT is measured and recorded, and we issue inspection records with the work.

Workshop and site

Smaller steel items come to our Al Sajaa workshop, where batches are blasted, coated, cured and checked. Tanks, pipework and structures are coated on site.

How we run a industrial coatings job

  1. Site survey. We inspect, measure and test on site before pricing anything.
  2. Quotation. Itemised scope, named system, method and programme. VAT shown separately.
  3. Execution. Our own crews, Monday to Saturday, phased around your operations.
  4. Handover. Joint inspection, snag closure and handover documents, with the warranty terms set out in the quotation.

Questions clients ask

Do you provide coating inspection records?

Yes. Dry-film thickness is checked and recorded, and on our steel bracket work a coating certificate is issued for every batch.

Do you visit the site before quoting?

Yes. We inspect, measure and test on site, then send an itemised quotation naming the system, method and programme. VAT is shown separately.

Can you work while our building or factory stays open?

Yes. We phase work around live operations, with barricades, signage and permits, and we can plan night or weekend shifts where needed.
