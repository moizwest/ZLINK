Civil Works

Civil works and concrete repair in Dubai and Sharjah

Concrete repair, crack injection, blockwork, drainage and demolition, done neatly around occupied spaces by our own civil team.

Get a quote+971 55 949 5833

Typical civil packages

Spalled and damaged concrete repaired with repair mortars, cracks sealed by resin injection, new blockwork and partitions, drainage replacement and controlled demolition ahead of refurbishment.

Part of a bigger job

Civil works often come before waterproofing, flooring or fit-out. Because those trades are also ours, one programme covers the sequence and nobody waits on another contractor.

Repairs that last

Before repairing concrete we break out loose and damaged material, treat exposed reinforcement and rebuild the section with repair mortar, so the repair bonds and does not spall again. Cracks are assessed first: live cracks and dormant cracks need different treatment.

How we run a civil works job

  1. Site survey. We inspect, measure and test on site before pricing anything.
  2. Quotation. Itemised scope, named system, method and programme. VAT shown separately.
  3. Execution. Our own crews, Monday to Saturday, phased around your operations.
  4. Handover. Joint inspection, snag closure and handover documents, with the warranty terms set out in the quotation.

Safe sites, every shift

Full PPE on every site, a daily toolbox talk before work starts, a risk assessment and method statement for every scope, and barricades, signage and permits around your operations.

Questions clients ask

Can you carry out drainage replacement in an occupied building?

Yes. We plan the work in sections with temporary arrangements so washrooms and facilities stay usable as far as possible.

Do you visit the site before quoting?

Yes. We inspect, measure and test on site, then send an itemised quotation naming the system, method and programme. VAT is shown separately.

Can you work while our building or factory stays open?

Yes. We phase work around live operations, with barricades, signage and permits, and we can plan night or weekend shifts where needed.
